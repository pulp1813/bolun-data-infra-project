# Sample data

| File | Rows | Used in | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `penguins.csv` | 344 | Lab 1 | Measurements of three penguin species on three islands in Antarctica, 2007–2009. Tidy, with a few missing values. |
| `penguins_raw.csv` | 344 | Lab 2 (optional) | The same study in its original form: long column names, extra fields, comments. Good practice for cleaning. |

**Source:** Palmer Station Antarctica LTER and Dr. Kristen Gorman, distributed through the
[palmerpenguins](https://allisonhorst.github.io/palmerpenguins/) project.
**License:** CC0 (public domain). Public, open data, so it is safe to use with free-tier AI tools.
